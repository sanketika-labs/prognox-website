Millions of learners generate events continuously — content opened, lessons
completed, assessments attempted. Understanding engagement across a country
means processing that stream as it arrives, not reconstructing it overnight.

Obsrv ingests and processes those events in real time, holding a 500M+ daily
average with peaks far above it. With UnQuery on top, programme officers ask
questions about district-level engagement directly, instead of queuing behind a
data team for every report.
