The platform was spending heavily on a pipeline that still could not keep up at
peak. Scaling the existing architecture meant paying more for a design that was
already the constraint.

Migrating to Obsrv doubled throughput and reduced annual infrastructure spend to
around $10K — roughly an 80% reduction. The gain came from replacing bespoke
processing jobs with configuration, which removed both the compute overhead and
the engineering time spent maintaining it.
